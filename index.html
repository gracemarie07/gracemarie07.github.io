ASTROPHYSICS · EXPLOSIVE TRANSIENTS · TIME-DOMAIN ASTRONOMY

I study how stars end—and what their explosions reveal.

I’m Grace Davis, an undergraduate researcher at Penn State’s Schreyer Honors College. I use optical and near-infrared observations to investigate supernova ejecta, radioactive mixing, and the changing universe.

Featured research

Inside a Type IIb supernova

My first-author study of SN 2025aico combines spectroscopy from 1.64 to 166.82 days after explosion with JWST mid-infrared imaging to examine radioactive mixing, asymmetric ejecta, and early dust emission.
