<div class="page-lead"><p class="eyebrow">ABOUT ME</p><h1>Science, service, and a curiosity that crosses disciplines.</h1></div>

I am an undergraduate at **The Pennsylvania State University’s Schreyer Honors College**, pursuing a B.S. in Astronomy and Astrophysics and a B.A. in International Politics, with a minor in American Sign Language. I expect to graduate in May 2028.

My research focuses on the rapidly changing universe. I am especially interested in what supernova spectra reveal about stellar explosions: how radioactive material is mixed through the ejecta, how the geometry of an explosion emerges in its emission lines, and how dust begins to form after core collapse. I also work with large time-domain surveys to identify rare transient events and changing-look active galactic nuclei.

I value science as both a technical and human endeavor. Alongside research, I mentor undergraduate students, help welcome new members of the Schreyer community, and organize fundraising and outreach through Penn State THON. My studies in international politics and American Sign Language strengthen my commitment to accessible, collaborative science.
