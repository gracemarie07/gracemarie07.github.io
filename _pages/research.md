<div class="page-lead"><p class="eyebrow">RESEARCH</p><h1>Reading the physics of cosmic explosions in light.</h1><p>I combine spectroscopy, time-domain surveys, and computational analysis to study how transient objects evolve.</p></div>

<section class="research-project" id="supernovae"><div class="project-number">01</div><div><p class="card-kicker">SUPERNOVAE · OPTICAL/NIR SPECTROSCOPY</p><h2>Probing Type IIb supernovae</h2><p>During an NSF REU at the University of Hawaiʻi Institute for Astronomy, I conducted remote observations with the Keck and NASA Infrared Telescope Facilities and analyzed optical and near-infrared spectroscopy of Type IIb supernovae.</p><p>I study how spectral lines evolve after a stellar explosion and what they reveal about the velocity, composition, and geometry of expanding ejecta. This work uses isolated near-infrared helium transitions to investigate radioactive mixing and the structure of helium-rich material.</p><ul class="tag-list"><li>Keck</li><li>NASA IRTF</li><li>Python</li><li>Spectral analysis</li></ul></div></section>

<section class="research-project"><div class="project-number">02</div><div><p class="card-kicker">TIME-DOMAIN ASTRONOMY · AGN · SURVEY SCIENCE</p><h2>Searching for changing-look active galactic nuclei</h2><p>With the Penn State Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics, I analyze Zwicky Transient Facility and Rubin Observatory survey data to identify unusual variability. I develop Python pipelines that track photometric behavior and help distinguish changing-look active galactic nuclei from other transient sources.</p><ul class="tag-list"><li>ZTF</li><li>Rubin Observatory</li><li>Photometry</li><li>Python pipelines</li></ul></div></section>

<section class="question-band"><p class="eyebrow">THE QUESTIONS THAT DRIVE ME</p><div class="question-grid"><p>How is radioactive material distributed through a stellar explosion?</p><p>What can line profiles tell us about the three-dimensional geometry of ejecta?</p><p>How can large surveys help us recognize rare astrophysical change?</p></div></section>
